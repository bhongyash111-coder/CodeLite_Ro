The Process

From Waste Listing
to Circular Value in 3 Steps

Register & List Waste

Factories register and list their waste outputs — material type, quantity, frequency, location, and availability window. Buyers list what inputs they need.

🤖

Our engine scores matches on material compatibility, proximity, volume overlap, and timing. Top matches surface in your dashboard with an environmental impact preview.
